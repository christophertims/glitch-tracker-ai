Glitch Tracker AI

Detect operational anomalies before they become costly financial incidents.

What we're building

Glitch Tracker AI is an AI-powered operational intelligence platform for financial institutions and fintechs.

The goal is to detect abnormal changes in transaction behaviour, latency, authentication, and operational workflows early, giving teams an opportunity to investigate before small problems become major incidents.

The problem

Financial systems do not always fail suddenly. Operational problems can begin as subtle changes in otherwise normal behaviour: rising transaction failures, unusual latency, authentication issues, or changes in workflow patterns.

Conventional monitoring often focuses on predefined errors and known failure conditions. Glitch Tracker AI explores a different approach: identifying meaningful deviations from normal operational behaviour as an early signal of potential problems.

Initial focus

The initial MVP hypothesis focuses on transaction-failure anomaly detection using signals such as transaction volume, success and failure rates, timestamps, and changes from established baselines.

Current stage

CONCEPT / PRE-MVP

Glitch Tracker AI is currently at the concept stage. The next step is to validate the problem with financial institutions and fintech operators and build an initial prototype based on that feedback.
